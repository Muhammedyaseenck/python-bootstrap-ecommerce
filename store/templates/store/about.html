A small store with a simple idea

Bazaar is an online store built as a Django and Bootstrap class project. It shows how a real shop is put together: a product database, pages that read from it, and a layout that works on every screen size.

Every product on the site is loaded from the Product table. Add, edit or remove items in the admin panel and the store updates straight away.

Fair prices

Every price is shown in rupees with no hidden charges.

Easy browsing

Search by name and move through pages of products at your own pace.

Works on any screen

Bootstrap's grid adapts the layout for phones, tablets and desktops.
